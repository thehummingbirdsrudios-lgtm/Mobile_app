## 1. The service

Vepari lets a jewellery business ("Business") manage its catalogue, customers,
orders, accounts (Hisaab), payments and bills, and share selected information
with its customers through apps such as WhatsApp. Accounts are created by the
Business owner; there is no public sign-up.

- The Business owner is responsible for creating staff accounts, setting their
