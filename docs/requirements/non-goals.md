# Non-goals (deliberately not built)

Per the product brief (§70–71), these are excluded until a real, frequent
vepari/owner problem justifies them:

- AI chatbot, AI recommendations (Regular Maal is a plain frequency list)
- Loyalty, referral, appointments
- Manufacturing, raw material, karigar management, full ERP
- Consumer marketplace / public storefront
- CRM pipelines, large analytics suites
- A separate staff app (the permission model allows one later)
- Offline-first sync engine (app is online-first; drafts are kept locally)
- Unofficial WhatsApp automation or scraping (only the platform share sheet
  now; WhatsApp Business API later behind its own module)
- Voice-to-text (voice notes stay original recordings)
- Inventory quantities (availability flag only for now)
